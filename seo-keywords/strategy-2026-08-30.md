## Exclusions / low fit

Do not optimize XJTLU pages for unrelated or materially different intent such as:

- `du học nghề trung quốc` and other vocational-study queries.
- one-year Chinese-language-only study queries unless a genuinely relevant XJTLU product exists.
- Korea-study queries that appeared in Keyword Planner suggestions.
- scholarship queries whose primary intent is government-funded study at traditional Chinese public universities unless the content can accurately satisfy that intent.

## Content architecture rule

Use one substantial China-study hub first instead of creating many thin pages for conditions, costs, scholarships and visa. Split a subsection into its own landing page only when Search Console shows meaningful impressions/query demand or when there is enough unique, authoritative content to satisfy the intent independently.

## GPT maintenance rules

1. Treat Keyword Planner data as market-demand evidence, not exact truth.
2. Before major content changes, inspect current Google SERP intent and ranking page types for the target query.
3. Verify changing facts (fees, scholarships, admission deadlines, rankings, visa rules) against official or primary sources before publishing.
4. Never replace XJTLU's own ranking with the ranking of University of Liverpool or Xi'an Jiaotong University; they are separate institutions for ranking purposes.
5. Use the homepage for brand/entity intent, the China-study hub for broad discovery intent, and focused pages for specific conversion intent.
6. After deployment, validate impressions, clicks, CTR and average position in Search Console; revise based on actual query/page data rather than guessed SEO metrics.
