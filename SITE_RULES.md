# XJTLU Vietnam site-specific operating notes

These notes are durable site-specific decisions for future edits.

- Verify factual claims with authoritative/official sources internally, but do **not** add a user-visible source box, citation footer, or source-links section on ordinary guide pages by default.
- Write ordinary XJTLU Vietnam guide pages as a direct Vietnamese local information site. State verified facts directly instead of using source-attribution wording such as `theo brochure`, `theo tài liệu chính thức`, `theo hướng dẫn`, `theo website chính thức`, or similar reporter/research phrasing.
- Keep official-source verification in the editorial/research process rather than showing source provenance in normal body copy.
- News articles are the main exception: preserve the original source and source link when editorially required.
- Do not expose Apply, How to apply, Application Portal, or generic university-homepage links by default. Prefer internal guides and TNS consultation paths.
